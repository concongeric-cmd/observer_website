## Research limits / 研究边界

The six active school profiles were checked against their official pages on 12 Nov 2026. Most descriptions are provider statements, not independent observation. Ochanomizu’s class capacity and staffing pages use different reference dates. Bing’s official pages conflict on what “36 children” counts. Greenspring’s official programme and admissions pages list different Children’s House age ranges. Unknown fields remain marked as uncollected and should not be read as absent services. National statistics use different definitions and should not be compared as if they measured the same service. / 六所当前园所档案于2026-11-12对照官方页面复核。大多数描述是园方自述，并非独立观察。御茶水女子大学附属幼稚园的班级定员与职员页面基准日期不同；Bing官方页面对“36名儿童”的统计范围说法不一致；Greenspring官方项目页与招生页对儿童之家年龄范围的表述不同。未知字段仍标记为尚未收集，不应理解为园所没有该服务。国家统计口径不同，不应视作同一服务指标直接比较。
